Beta Program Rewards & Expectations

What participating in the RebataTrack Beta Program means.

Beta access is limited, so we want each spot to be used by someone who is genuinely participating in the testing process.

What We Ask From Beta Testers

Beta testers are expected to:

  • Actively use RebataTrack throughout the beta period.
  • Test as many areas and features of the app as reasonably possible.
  • Use RebataTrack with real rebate activity when possible.
  • Report bugs, crashes, confusing behavior, or anything that does not work as expected.
  • Provide honest feedback, including both what you like and what you think could be improved.
  • Share suggestions for features, improvements, or changes that could make RebataTrack more useful.
  • Complete periodic required testing tasks assigned through the RebataTrack Beta Portal by their stated deadlines.
  • Keep the beta version reasonably up to date so you are testing the latest available build.

You do not need to find a certain number of bugs or submit feedback every day. We simply ask that beta testers genuinely use the app and participate in helping us improve it.

Limited Beta Availability

Beta access is limited, and we expect interest to exceed the number of spots available.

Beta spots that are not being actively used may be reassigned to another person on the waitlist.

If you stop using RebataTrack for an extended period or do not participate in testing, your beta access may be removed so another interested tester can participate.

This is not intended to penalize anyone. It simply helps us make the best use of the limited beta availability.

What Beta Testing Means

Beta software is still being tested and improved. During the beta, you may encounter:

  • Bugs or unexpected behavior
  • Features that change between versions
  • Design or wording changes
  • Features that are added, removed, or redesigned
  • Occasional crashes or performance issues
  • Test data or workflows that may need to be reset as development continues

Finding these issues is exactly why the beta exists. We encourage testers to report anything that feels confusing, inconvenient, incorrect, or difficult to use — even if you are not sure whether it is technically a bug.

Honest Feedback Is Encouraged

You will never lose beta access because you gave negative feedback. We want honest opinions.

If something is confusing, tell us. If you dislike a feature, tell us. If something takes too many steps, tell us. If there is something you think RebataTrack desperately needs, tell us.

Positive feedback is appreciated, but constructive criticism is often what helps improve the app the most.

Our Commitment to Beta Testers

In return, we will do our best to:

  • Listen carefully to tester feedback.
  • Investigate reported bugs and issues.
  • Continue improving the app throughout the beta.
  • Communicate important changes when new beta versions are released.
  • Take suggestions seriously while balancing the needs of the overall RebataTrack community.

Not every suggestion will necessarily become a feature, but every thoughtful piece of feedback is valuable.

Beta Tester Participation Reward

Testers who actively participate throughout the RebataTrack Beta Program can qualify for 3 complimentary months of RebataTrack+ after RebataTrack’s public launch.

Active participation means genuinely using RebataTrack, completing periodic testing tasks assigned through the Beta Portal by their required deadlines, and sharing meaningful feedback that helps us improve the experience.

The reward is intended for testers who meaningfully participate — not simply everyone who applies, is approved, or receives beta access. Honest constructive criticism is encouraged and qualification is not based on whether feedback is positive.

The complimentary RebataTrack+ reward has no cash value, is non-transferable, and cannot be sold, traded, exchanged, redeemed for cash or credit, or substituted for another benefit. Eligibility and fulfillment are subject to the Beta Program Terms and the availability of RebataTrack+ following public launch.

Thank You for Helping Build RebataTrack

RebataTrack was created by a rebater to solve the problems that come with managing purchases, obligations, deadlines, spending, and refunds.

Beta testers are an important part of making sure RebataTrack works just as well for other rebaters in the real world.

By participating, you are helping shape RebataTrack before it reaches the wider rebate community.

Thank you for being part of the beginning.
