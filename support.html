Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my Rebatify account?

Account deletion is built directly into Rebatify. Open More → Account & Security and tap Delete Rebatify Account. The confirmation screen requires your current password and asks you to type DELETE before the destructive action is enabled. Deletion permanently removes your personal Rebatify profile, orders, archive, setup information, and account data. Rebatify may retain trial-eligibility records so deleting an account cannot restart the 14-day trial. If you own a shared Rebatify+ group, deleting your account ends that shared group, but other members keep their own personal profiles and data.

What does “Cash Still Tied Up” / pending refunds mean?

It is the refund money Rebatify still actively expects to recover from active orders. It intentionally excludes expected out-of-pocket cost. If an order is completed with a partial refund, the unrecovered amount remains part of that order’s historical actual-Out Of Pocket Expense result, but it stops counting as pending cash.

Can I record more than one refund on the same order?

Yes. Refunds are stored as transactions, so you can record additional refunds later. The order’s “Refunded So Far,” “Actual Out Of Pocket Expense,” and “Outstanding Refund” update from the recorded refund transactions.

What happens when I receive only part of the expected refund?

You can record the partial amount and continue waiting for additional refund transactions. If you decide the remaining amount will not be recovered, Rebatify can complete the order with a partial refund after the required order workflow is complete. That action approves the financial shortfall only—it does not bypass missing delivery, form, review, rating, seller-feedback, or other applicable workflow steps.

How does Group Refund work?

Open Orders → Group Refund. Enter the actual amount received; that amount is the entire allocation pool. Choose the refund method/account and select every order that was included in the transaction. Rebatify compares the selected orders’ outstanding expected refunds with the amount actually received and automatically allocates that pool proportionally across the selected orders. Cent-level rounding is reconciled so the allocations equal the received transaction exactly.

Does Group Refund subtract a fee before allocating?

No. Group Refund asks for the actual amount that arrived, and that entered amount becomes the full allocation pool. If a third party withheld a fee before paying you, enter the amount that was actually received.

What does a Source of Rebate Deals control automatically?

A Source of Rebate Deals controls the order's Refund Due / Reminder Timeline. Rebatify calculates the expected refund due date automatically from the selected source and the order date. A source can use the Master Default Refund Timeline or its own custom number of days. Refund method/account and obligation choices still remain order-specific.

What is the Master Default Refund Timeline?

It is the default refund timeline used when a Source of Rebate Deals does not have its own custom setting. By default, Rebatify expects refunds 7 days after the order date. That same timeline controls when refund reminders begin and when a refund becomes due or overdue. You can change it in More → Reminders & Notification Settings → Reminder Settings.

When does an order move to Archive?

Rebatify requires both sides of the order to be ready: the applicable workflow must be complete and the refund requirement must be satisfied. A full refund does not bypass an incomplete order workflow. Once every applicable workflow step is complete and the refund is fully satisfied—or you have explicitly completed the order with a partial refund—the order can automatically archive. Editing an archived order so it becomes incomplete can reopen it.

What is included in the 14-day trial?

The trial provides basic app access for 14 days. Rebatify+ premium features—advanced reporting, premium reminders/notifications, and shared profiles—remain locked during the basic-access trial. When the trial ends, Rebatify+ is required to continue using the full app.

What happens to my data when the trial ends?

Your account is not treated as though the data disappeared. The app provides a basic CSV export option from the trial-ended screen containing basic order information such as order number, date, source, item, order amount, refund amount, and basic statuses. Rebatify+ restores continued app access and premium reporting/export capability.

What does Rebatify+ add?

Rebatify+ provides continued app access after the trial plus advanced Financial Intelligence reporting, report-library views, custom workflow reminder timing, notification controls, shared membership for up to three users/profiles, and web access from a supported browser. Each profile keeps separate orders and setup data.

Can I use Rebatify on the web?

Yes. Web access is included with Rebatify+. You can access Rebatify from a supported web browser in addition to your iPhone. Your Rebatify account stays synchronized across authorized clients, and web access counts as one of the trusted device/client slots for that login.

How do shared Rebatify+ profiles work?

A Rebatify+ shared membership supports up to 3 users. The owner generates an invite code, and the other person signs in with their own Rebatify account before joining. Switching profiles changes the Rebatify data view; each profile keeps separate orders, sources, shopping/ordering accounts, refund accounts, reports, and setup data. Invite codes expire after 7 days.

Are device restrictions shared between Rebatify+ members?

No. Trusted-device protection stays tied to each person’s own login account. Device protection is tied to each person’s own login. Standard access uses one primary-device slot. With Rebatify+, each login can use up to 3 trusted devices/clients total, with web access counting as one of those slots. Replacement protection uses a 24-hour cooldown and 48-hour takeover/promotion timing when applicable.

How does cloud sync work if I lose connection?

Rebatify saves changes on the device first. When cloud access is available, those changes upload to synchronize authorized devices. If the connection is unavailable, you can continue working with locally saved changes and Rebatify retries cloud upload automatically when connectivity returns.

How do I change reminder or notification types?

These are Rebatify+ features. Open More → Reminders & Notification Settings. Reminder Settings controls the Master Default Refund Timeline plus Review & Rating, Delivery Confirmation, and Pending Review Check timing. Notifications can be individually enabled for Review & Rating Reminders, Delivery Confirmation, Pending Review Checks, and Refund Due & Overdue Refunds.

What does the first-time setup ask me to create?

First-time setup walks through five key items in order: Shopping/Ordering Account, Master Default Refund Timeline, Main Source of Rebate Deals, Refund Account, and Tax Preference. The first source can inherit the master refund timeline or use one source-specific number of days.

What should I send when reporting a bug?

Include your Rebatify app version, iPhone model, iOS version, the screen or order involved, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and a screenshot if it helps. Do not email passwords or other authentication secrets.

Can’t access the app? In-app account deletion is the normal path when you can sign in. If you cannot access your account and need help with a privacy or deletion request, email support.rebatifyapp@gmail.com.
