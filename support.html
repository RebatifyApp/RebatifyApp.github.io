Frequently asked questions

How do I add a rebate purchase?

Create a new order and enter the purchase details, expected refund information, rebate source, refund method, and any notes that apply to that specific order.

Can an order have a partial refund?

Yes. Rebatify is designed to support partial refunds. A completed order may preserve any unrecovered amount as actual out-of-pocket variance instead of continuing to show it as an actively expected refund.

What does “Cash Still Tied Up” mean?

It is intended to represent refund money that is still actively expected from open orders. It is not simply the total purchase amount minus all refunds received.

Can one refund transaction cover multiple orders?

Yes. Rebatify may allow a received refund amount to be associated with multiple selected orders and allocated according to the applicable expected refund amounts.

What should I do if my data looks incorrect?

First verify the order amount, expected refund, recorded refund transactions, fees, and completion status. If the issue remains, contact support with the affected order name or identifier and a description of what you expected to see.

How do I request deletion of my account or data?

Contact support from the email address associated with your account and request account or data deletion. We may need to verify that you control the account before completing the request.

How do I report a bug?

Please include the device model, iOS version, Rebatify app version, what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and screenshots when appropriate.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm you are using the latest version of Rebatify available to you.
  • Close and reopen the app if a screen appears stuck or stale.
  • Confirm you are signed into the intended account or profile.
  • Do not send passwords, full payment card numbers, bank credentials, or other sensitive authentication information by email.

App Store billing

If a subscription or in-app purchase is processed through Apple, billing, cancellation, and refund eligibility may also be governed by Apple’s App Store rules and your Apple account settings. Rebatify cannot directly change Apple account credentials or Apple’s billing records.

Response information

We aim to review support requests as reasonably practical. Response times can vary depending on request volume, issue complexity, weekends, holidays, and whether additional information is needed.
