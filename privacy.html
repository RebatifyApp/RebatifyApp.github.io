Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026

App account deletion: Rebatify includes account deletion directly in the app at More → Account & Security → Delete Rebatify Account. Support is available when a user cannot access the app.

This Privacy Policy explains how Rebatify (“Rebatify,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) may collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you use the Rebatify mobile application, website, Rebatify+ features, and support services (collectively, the “Services”).

1. Information We May Collect

Account and profile information

We may process your name, email address, account identifiers, authentication information handled through our authentication systems, selected profile, subscription or entitlement status, and account settings.

Order, rebate, and refund information

Information you enter may include order numbers, product names, platforms or sellers, purchase amounts, taxes, shipping, fees, discounts, expected refunds, refund transactions, transaction identifiers, expected delivery/refund dates, obligation status, order notes, rebate sources, shopping/ordering accounts, refund methods, refund-account labels, OOP rules, and archive/history information.

Shared Rebatify+ information

If you use Rebatify+ shared profiles, we may process shared-membership identifiers, profile membership, owner/member status, selected profile, and invite-code information needed to join or administer the shared membership. Each user’s personal profile data remains logically separate from other profiles within the shared membership.

Device and technical information

We may process device identifiers and device-trust information needed for primary-device protection, app/version details, operating-system information, sync state, diagnostics, crash/error information, and network-related data required to operate and secure the Services.

Support communications

If you contact support, we may process your email address, message, attachments, and the information you voluntarily provide.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate accounts, profiles, order tracking, refund tracking, grouped-refund allocation, archive, and reporting.
  • Save changes locally and synchronize supported data to cloud storage.
  • Calculate expected refund, actual refund, expected OOP, actual OOP, pending refund, refund aging, and related metrics.
  • Operate Rebatify+ reminders, notifications, advanced reports, and shared profiles when enabled.
  • Maintain trusted-device rules, prevent repeated trial resets, protect accounts, and investigate misuse or security problems.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Local-First Saving and Cloud Sync

Rebatify is designed to save changes on the device first. When cloud access is available, supported changes are uploaded so authorized devices can synchronize. If cloud connectivity is unavailable, locally saved work can remain available and cloud upload may retry automatically when connectivity returns.

4. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell personal information in the ordinary meaning of “sell.” Information may be disclosed to service providers that help operate authentication, cloud infrastructure, app distribution/subscription services, diagnostics, or support; when you direct sharing through a Rebatify+ invite or profile feature; where required by law; or in connection with a business transaction such as a merger or asset sale, subject to applicable law.

5. Rebatify+ Shared Profiles

A Rebatify+ shared membership may contain up to three users. Each person signs in with their own account. Switching profiles changes the visible Rebatify data context, and each profile keeps separate order and setup data. Device protection remains tied to each person’s own login account.

6. Trial Eligibility and Device Protection

Rebatify uses device/account information to administer the 14-day basic-access trial and trusted-device protections. Trial-eligibility records may be retained after account deletion so deleting and recreating an account does not restart the trial. The current app maintains one primary device per account, allows temporary access movement under its device rules, and may promote a new device to primary after the applicable continuous-use period.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical, organizational, and administrative safeguards designed to protect information. No electronic transmission, device, or storage platform can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Services, preserve user-requested history, comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or trial abuse, enforce agreements, and maintain security. Different information may have different retention periods.

9. Account Deletion and Your Rights

Rebatify provides an in-app deletion flow. Open More → Account & Security → Delete Rebatify Account. The current flow requires the account password and confirmation text before deletion. Deleting your account removes your personal Rebatify profile, orders, archive, setup information, and account data, subject to limited records that may be retained for legal, security, or trial-eligibility purposes. If you own a shared Rebatify+ group, deleting your account ends that shared group; other members keep their own personal profiles.

If you cannot access the app, contact support.rebatifyapp@gmail.com. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of certain personal information.

10. California Privacy Information

California residents may have additional rights under applicable California privacy laws. These may include rights to know, access, correct, or delete certain personal information and to receive information about certain disclosures. Rebatify will not discriminate against a user for exercising rights provided by applicable law.

11. Children’s Privacy

Rebatify is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 in violation of applicable law.

12. Third-Party Services

The Services may rely on third-party providers for functions such as authentication, cloud infrastructure, App Store purchases, or diagnostics. Their processing may be governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Services change. We may update the effective date and provide additional notice when appropriate.

14. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to support.rebatifyapp@gmail.com.
