Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Important: This policy is written as a thorough general privacy policy for Rebatify. Before App Store release, update any bracket-free assumptions so they exactly match the app’s real data practices, service providers, analytics tools, authentication system, and support email.

This Privacy Policy explains how Rebatify (“Rebatify,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) may collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you use the Rebatify mobile application, related websites, support channels, and services (collectively, the “Services”).

1. Information We May Collect

Account and profile information

When account functionality is available, we may collect information such as your name, email address, account identifier, authentication information supplied through our authentication provider, profile names, and settings.

Order and rebate information

The Services are designed to let users record information relating to purchases and expected or received rebates and refunds. Depending on how you use the app, this may include merchant or source names, item descriptions, purchase amounts, expected refund amounts, refund amounts, fees, dates, order identifiers, payment or refund method labels, notes, completion status, and other information you choose to enter.

Financial-related information

Rebatify may store user-entered financial tracking information such as purchase values, expected reimbursements, refunds received, fees, and calculated out-of-pocket values. Unless the app specifically provides a secure payment-account integration, you should not enter full payment card numbers, online banking passwords, security codes, or similar financial credentials into free-text fields.

Device and technical information

We may receive technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, language, crash information, diagnostic information, IP address, and other information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services.

Support communications

If you contact us, we may collect your email address, message content, attachments, diagnostic details, and any other information you choose to provide.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve Rebatify.
  • Create and manage user accounts and profiles.
  • Store and display rebate, purchase, refund, and reporting information entered by users.
  • Calculate expected refunds, received refunds, open refund amounts, out-of-pocket amounts, fees, and related app metrics.
  • Synchronize information across supported devices when cloud features are enabled.
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, misuse, abuse, security incidents, or technical problems.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.
  • Analyze product performance and improve features, reliability, and usability.

3. Legal Bases and Consent

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we process information as necessary to provide the Services you request, based on your consent where required, to comply with legal obligations, and for legitimate interests such as maintaining security, preventing abuse, improving the Services, and supporting users.

4. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell your personal information in the ordinary meaning of “sell.” We may disclose information in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers. We may use third parties for hosting, cloud storage, authentication, analytics, crash reporting, customer support, email delivery, subscriptions, and infrastructure.
  • At your direction. Information may be shared when you choose to share, export, invite, connect, or otherwise direct us to make information available to another person or service.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, regulation, legal process, court order, or governmental request, or to protect rights, safety, security, and property.
  • Business transfers. Information may be transferred in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction, subject to applicable law.

5. Shared or Multi-User Features

If Rebatify provides shared access, invited users, or switchable profiles, information available to another person may depend on the permissions, account structure, and profile selected within the app. You are responsible for deciding whom to invite and for protecting invitation codes or other access credentials.

6. Subscriptions and App Store Purchases

Purchases made through Apple’s App Store are processed by Apple. We may receive limited transaction-related information, such as subscription status, entitlement status, product identifier, purchase validation information, or transaction identifiers. We generally do not receive your full payment card information from Apple.

7. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. However, no electronic transmission, device, network, or storage system is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, email account, login credentials, and any authentication method associated with your account.

8. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain account and transaction history, comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, enforce agreements, and support legitimate business operations. Retention periods may vary based on the type of information and the reason it is processed.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information. You may also have rights to restrict or object to certain processing or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To submit a privacy request, contact support.rebatifyapp@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity or account ownership before fulfilling a request.

10. California Privacy Information

California residents may have additional rights under applicable California privacy laws. Depending on whether those laws apply to Rebatify and the information involved, these rights may include the right to know or access categories and specific pieces of personal information, request correction or deletion, and receive information about certain disclosures. Rebatify will not discriminate against a user for exercising privacy rights provided by applicable law.

If Rebatify ever engages in activity legally defined as “selling” or “sharing” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, this policy and any required opt-out mechanisms will be updated accordingly.

11. Children’s Privacy

Rebatify is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children under 13. If we learn that personal information was collected from a child in a manner prohibited by applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

12. International Use

If you use the Services outside the country where our systems or providers are located, information may be processed or stored in another jurisdiction. Applicable safeguards will be used where required by law.

13. Third-Party Services and Links

The Services may contain links to or integrations with third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review the privacy terms of any third-party service you use.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we may update the effective date above and, when appropriate, provide additional notice within the app or through another reasonable method.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, contact us at support.rebatifyapp@gmail.com.
