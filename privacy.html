Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

App account deletion: RebataTrack includes account deletion directly in the app at More → Account & Security → Delete RebataTrack Account. RebataTrack Beta Program records are administered separately; contact support if you want to request access to or deletion of Beta Program records.

This Privacy Policy explains how RebataTrack (“RebataTrack,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) may collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you use the RebataTrack mobile application, website, RebataTrack+ features, RebataTrack Beta Program, Beta Portal, and support services (collectively, the “Services”).

1. Information We May Collect

Account and profile information

We may process your name, email address, account identifiers, authentication information handled through our authentication systems, selected profile, subscription or entitlement status, account settings, and related account-security information.

Order, rebate, and refund information

Information you enter may include order numbers, product names, platforms or sellers, purchase amounts, taxes, shipping, fees, discounts, expected refunds, refund transactions, transaction identifiers, expected delivery information, calculated expected refund due dates, source refund-timeline settings, obligation status, order notes, rebate sources, shopping or ordering accounts, refund methods, refund-account labels, Out Of Pocket Expense rules, and archive or history information.

Shared RebataTrack+ information

If you use RebataTrack+ shared profiles, we may process shared-membership identifiers, profile membership, owner/member status, selected profile, and invite-code information needed to join or administer the shared membership. Each user’s personal profile data remains logically separate from other profiles within the shared membership.

Device and technical information

We may process device identifiers and device-trust information needed for primary-device protection, app/version details, operating-system information, sync state, diagnostics, crash/error information, security events, and network-related data required to operate and secure the Services.

RebataTrack Beta Program information

If you apply for or participate in the RebataTrack Beta Program, we may process your name, approved email address, selected testing platform, application and access status, administrative notes, tester identifiers, portal sign-in, last-login and portal-activity timestamps, inactivity status, assigned testing tasks, task deadlines, completion status, task responses, reminder history, feedback submissions and retest responses, the app build number reported with Help & Feedback conversations, device model, operating-system version, automatically detected browser screen-size details, confirmation of the approved Apple Account or Google Play account used for beta distribution, Beta Program announcements and acknowledgement records, program communications, and records reasonably needed to administer participation and any Beta Program reward eligibility.

Support communications

If you contact support, we may process your email address, message, attachments, and other information you voluntarily provide.

Please do not submit highly sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, government identification numbers, full payment-card credentials, financial-account passwords, or authentication secrets in notes, Help & Feedback conversations, or support messages.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate accounts, profiles, order tracking, refund tracking, grouped-refund allocation, archive, reporting, and RebataTrack+ functionality.
  • Save changes locally and synchronize supported data to cloud storage.
  • Calculate expected refund, actual refund, expected Out Of Pocket Expense, actual Out Of Pocket Expense, pending refund, refund aging, and related metrics.
  • Operate Beta Program applications, portal access, required Testing Setup, testing assignments, deadlines, reminders, feedback status updates, retest requests, portal announcements, participation status, tester communications, and reward-qualification administration.
  • Maintain trusted-device rules, prevent repeated trial resets, protect accounts, detect abuse, and investigate security problems.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests, enforce our terms, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Beta Portal Authentication and Session Security

The Beta Portal uses passwordless email verification. When a tester requests access, a short-lived 6-digit verification code is sent to the approved beta email. Temporary verification records are designed to expire automatically after a short period and are used only to complete the sign-in process and protect against abuse. Successful Beta Portal authentication is recorded as the tester’s last login for program administration and security. For security, an authenticated Beta Portal session automatically ends after approximately 6 hours of inactivity, after which the tester must request a new verification code.

4. On-Device Saving and Cloud Sync

RebataTrack is designed to save changes on the device first. When cloud access is available, supported changes are uploaded so authorized devices can synchronize. If cloud connectivity is unavailable, locally saved work can remain available and cloud upload may retry automatically when connectivity returns.

5. Service Providers and Disclosures

We may disclose information to service providers that perform functions on our behalf and only as reasonably necessary to provide or protect the Services. Depending on the feature used, these providers may include Google/Firebase for authentication and databases, Cloudflare for Beta Program backend processing and temporary verification data, GitHub Pages for website delivery, Google email services for transactional Beta Program emails, Apple/TestFlight for iOS beta distribution, and Google Play for Android beta distribution and app delivery. We may change service providers as the Services evolve.

Information may also be disclosed when you direct sharing through a RebataTrack+ invite or profile feature, when required by law or legal process, to protect rights or security, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

6. No Sale or Cross-Context Behavioral Advertising

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under applicable California privacy law. We do not use Beta Program application, task, or feedback data for targeted advertising.

7. RebataTrack+ Shared Profiles

A RebataTrack+ shared membership may contain up to three users. Each person signs in with their own account. Switching profiles changes the RebataTrack information shown in the app, and each profile keeps separate order and setup data. Device protection remains tied to each person’s own login account.

8. Trial Eligibility and Device Protection

RebataTrack uses device/account information to administer the 14-day basic-access trial and trusted-device protections. Trial-eligibility records may be retained after account deletion so deleting and recreating an account does not restart the trial. Standard access maintains one primary-device access slot. RebataTrack+ may support up to three trusted devices/clients per login, including web access as a trusted slot. Device records may use Primary, Trusted, Temporary, and Not Active states, with 24-hour replacement-cooldown and 48-hour takeover/promotion protections where applicable.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical, organizational, and administrative safeguards designed to protect information, including access controls, authentication, limited-duration verification codes, encrypted transport where supported, and service-provider security features. No electronic transmission, device, website, cloud service, or storage platform can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including operating the Services, maintaining user-requested history, administering the Beta Program and any tester reward, resolving disputes, preventing fraud or trial abuse, enforcing agreements, maintaining security, and complying with law. Different categories may have different retention periods.

Beta Program verification codes and associated temporary authentication records are designed to expire quickly. Beta application, task, Help & Feedback conversations, access, and participation records may be retained for the duration of the Beta Program and for a reasonable period afterward to administer eligibility, document program activity, investigate abuse, resolve disputes, or fulfill any earned benefit. Feedback that has been incorporated into product development may be retained in aggregated, de-identified, or non-identifying form.

11. Account Deletion and Privacy Requests

RebataTrack provides an in-app deletion flow for the RebataTrack app account. Open More → Account & Security → Delete RebataTrack Account. Deleting the app account removes personal RebataTrack app data subject to limited records that may be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or trial-eligibility purposes.

The RebataTrack app account and the RebataTrack Beta Program administration system are separate. Deleting the app account does not necessarily delete Beta Program applications, tasks, Help & Feedback conversations, participation records, or portal-administration records. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of Beta Program records, contact support.rebatifyapp@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

12. California Privacy Information

If and to the extent California privacy law applies to RebataTrack, California residents may have rights to know or access certain personal information, request correction or deletion, receive information about certain disclosures, opt out of sale or sharing, limit certain uses of sensitive personal information where applicable, and exercise rights without unlawful discrimination. Because RebataTrack does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, we do not currently offer personal information for those purposes.

13. Children and Beta Program Age Eligibility

RebataTrack is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 in violation of applicable law. The RebataTrack Beta Program is intended only for adults who are at least 18 years old or the age of majority in their jurisdiction. If we learn that a Beta Program participant does not meet this requirement, we may remove their Beta Program access.

14. Third-Party Platforms and Links

Apple, TestFlight, Google Play, Firebase, Cloudflare, GitHub, email providers, and other third-party services have their own privacy practices and terms. When you interact directly with those services, their policies may also apply. RebataTrack is not responsible for third-party privacy practices outside our control.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Services, Beta Program, legal requirements, or service providers change. We will update the effective date and may provide additional notice for material changes when appropriate.

16. Contact

Privacy questions and requests may be sent to support.rebatifyapp@gmail.com.

Beta Help & Feedback

During the beta, RebataTrack may store the support requests, feedback reports, conversation replies, status updates, retest responses, device/build context, and account-mismatch information you voluntarily submit through the Beta Portal. These records are used to operate beta support, investigate reports, communicate with testers, and improve RebataTrack.

Account and access support requests. Testers may submit account/access support from the Beta Portal sign-in page when they cannot sign in. We use the approved beta email, selected issue type, and message to identify the beta record, respond to the request, and restore or correct access when appropriate.
