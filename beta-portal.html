How To

Quick RebataTrack guides.

Open only the guide you need.

OrdersHow do I add a new order?
  1. Open Orders from the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap Add New Order.
  3. Enter the purchase and rebate information, then save the order.

The Orders screen is also where you can search existing orders and start a multi-order refund.

Order DetailsHow do I review or update an order?
  1. Open Orders.
  2. Tap the order you want to review.
  3. Use Order Details to review its timeline, amounts, status, and next action.
RefundsHow do I record a refund?
  1. Open the applicable order.
  2. Scroll to Record Refund.
  3. Enter the refund amount and optional transaction ID, then record it.

If only part of the expected refund has arrived, RebataTrack keeps the remaining balance visible while the order workflow continues.

Reports · RebataTrack+How do I see what I have spent or received?
  1. Tap Reports.
  2. Choose the period or report you want to review.
  3. Use the totals and breakdowns to understand spending, refunds, and Out Of Pocket Expense.

Reports is a RebataTrack+ perk. If you do not have RebataTrack+ active yet, activate it first to unlock the full Reports experience shown here.

Shared Profiles · RebataTrack+How do I use a shared profile?
  1. Open More.
  2. Open Shared Profiles.
  3. Create or join the appropriate shared profile, then follow the on-screen invitation flow.

Shared Profiles is a RebataTrack+ perk. You will need RebataTrack+ activated to use shared-profile features and invitations.

RebataTrack+How do I upgrade to RebataTrack+?

Use the path that matches what you see in RebataTrack. The screenshots below are from the iPhone app; Android follows the same RebataTrack+ flow and completes the purchase through Google Play instead of the App Store.

Trial still active

Open More and choose View Plans.

  1. Tap More in the bottom navigation.
  2. Find the RebataTrack+ card and tap View Plans.
  3. Choose Monthly or Annual.
  4. Confirm the purchase using the App Store or Google Play purchase sheet.
Trial already ended

Choose a plan directly from the trial-ended screen.

  1. On Your RebataTrack trial has ended, scroll to Available RebataTrack+ Subscriptions.
  2. Tap Monthly or Annual.
  3. Confirm the purchase through the App Store or Google Play.
  4. If you already purchased RebataTrack+ but access is not showing, use Restore Purchases.

Note: The App Store or Google Play confirmation screen is the authoritative place for the current price, billing period, and purchase confirmation.

Android Beta AccessHow do I install RebataTrack from Google Play beta testing?
  1. On your Android phone, open the Google Play Store.
  2. Tap your profile picture and make sure Google Play is using the Google Account that matches your approved RebataTrack Beta email.
  3. After RebataTrack marks your testing access as sent, return to the Your Beta Build card near the top of this portal and tap Open Google Play Testing Link.
  4. On Google’s testing page, choose the option to join / become a tester.
  5. Open the Google Play listing from the confirmation page and install RebataTrack.
  6. Open RebataTrack, sign in or create your app account with your approved beta email, then return here for required tasks and feedback.

If Google says the test is unavailable, verify that both Google Play and the browser opening the link are using your approved Google Account. Do not submit a second beta application; use Help & Feedback → Account / Access Problem if the Google Account email needs to be corrected.

MoreWhere do I find account, reminder, and device settings?
  1. Tap More in the bottom navigation.
  2. Choose the section you need, such as accounts, archives, reminders, notifications, security, or device management.
  3. Use Help & Learning lower on the page for support, policies, and the guided walkthrough.
FAQ

Beta tester questions.

How do I add a new order?

Open Orders, then tap Add New Order. Enter the purchase and rebate information shown in the form and save. See the How To section above for the exact Orders screen.

How do I find an order I already entered?

Open Orders and use the search field. You can search using details such as the item, order number, source, notes, or other identifying information shown by the app.

How do I see what an order is waiting on?

Open the order from the Orders list. Its Order Details screen shows the current status, timeline, expected refund information, and the next action that needs attention.

How do I record a refund that covers more than one order?

Open Orders and choose Record a Multi-Order Refund. RebataTrack requires multiple eligible orders for this workflow, so a single order cannot be submitted as a multi-order refund.

How do I view spending, refunds, and Out Of Pocket Expense?

Open Reports. The Reports area gives you time-period views and breakdowns of spending, refunds, and Out Of Pocket Expense based on your recorded orders.

Are Reports and Shared Profiles included for every tester?

No. Reports and Shared Profiles are RebataTrack+ perks. If those sections are locked, activate RebataTrack+ first.

How do I upgrade to RebataTrack+?

If your trial is still active, open More, find the RebataTrack+ card, tap View Plans, choose Monthly or Annual, and confirm through the App Store or Google Play. If your trial has already ended, choose Monthly or Annual directly from the Available RebataTrack+ Subscriptions section on the trial-ended screen. See the screenshot guide in How To above for both paths.

How do Android testers install RebataTrack from beta testing?

Use the Google Account that matches your approved RebataTrack Beta email. After the RebataTrack team sends your Android testing access, the Your Beta Build card in this portal displays Open Google Play Testing Link. Open that link on your Android phone, join/become a tester on Google’s page, then open the Play listing and install RebataTrack. The same steps and testing link are also sent to your approved beta email.

How do I submit beta feedback?

Use the Tell us what happened form above. Include the page or feature and the required RebataTrack Build number shown at the bottom of More → Settings. Enter only the number itself (for example, 228). Your saved device, OS, and screen-size details are attached automatically.

What if I signed up with the wrong email and it does not match my Apple Account or Google Play account?

If the email on your RebataTrack Beta profile is not the Apple Account you use for the App Store/TestFlight on iPhone or iPad, or the Google Account selected in Google Play on Android, do not submit a new application. Open Help & Feedback, choose Account / Access Problem, and send us the correct account email. The RebataTrack team can update your existing beta profile so your application, testing history, conversations, and access stay together.

Do iPhone and iPad testers need TestFlight installed?

Yes. If you are approved for iOS testing, install the TestFlight app on the iPhone or iPad you will use for RebataTrack before completing Testing Setup. That device must be signed in with the Apple Account whose email matches your approved RebataTrack Beta email. If the emails do not match, use Help & Feedback → Account / Access Problem so we can correct your beta email before you continue.

How often do I need to submit feedback?

There is no daily quota. We simply ask that you genuinely use RebataTrack during the beta and submit meaningful feedback when you encounter an issue or have a useful suggestion.

Can I report something even if I am not sure it is a bug?

Yes. If something feels confusing, inconvenient, inconsistent, or difficult to use, submit it. That kind of feedback is valuable even when the software is technically working as designed.

Will negative feedback affect my beta access?

No. Honest feedback is encouraged. Constructive criticism is one of the main reasons the beta exists.

What happens if I stop testing?

Because beta availability is limited, inactive spots may eventually be reassigned to another person on the waitlist.

How do I sign in to the RebataTrack Beta Portal?

Use the same email address that was approved for the RebataTrack Beta Program. We will send a 6-digit verification code to that email. The portal does not use a separate password. If you cannot receive the code or need your portal access reset, use Need help signing in? on the Beta Portal sign-in page to open an account/access support request.
