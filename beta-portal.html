FAQ

Beta tester questions.

How often do I need to submit feedback?

There is no daily quota. We simply ask that you genuinely use Rebatify during the beta and submit meaningful feedback when you encounter an issue or have a useful suggestion.

Can I report something even if I am not sure it is a bug?

Yes. If something feels confusing, inconvenient, inconsistent, or difficult to use, submit it. That kind of feedback is valuable even when the software is technically working as designed.

Will negative feedback affect my beta access?

No. Honest feedback is encouraged. Constructive criticism is one of the main reasons the beta exists.

What happens if I stop testing?

Because beta availability is limited, inactive spots may eventually be reassigned to another person on the waitlist.

How do I get help with my portal login?

Email support.rebatifyapp@gmail.com if you cannot sign in or need your portal access reset.
