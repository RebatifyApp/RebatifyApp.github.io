How To

Quick guides for the main Rebatify workflows.

These guides use actual Rebatify screens so you can quickly see where to go. More step-by-step screenshots will be added as beta workflows expand.

Orders

How do I add a new order?

  1. Open Orders from the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap Add New Order.
  3. Enter the purchase and rebate information, then save the order.

The same Orders screen is also where you can search existing orders and start a multi-order refund.

Order Details

How do I review or update an order?

  1. Open Orders.
  2. Tap the order you want to review.
  3. Use the Order Details screen to review its timeline, amounts, status, and next action.
Reports

How do I see what I have spent or received?

  1. Tap Reports in the bottom navigation.
  2. Choose the period or report you want to review.
  3. Use the totals and breakdowns to understand spending, refunds, and Out Of Pocket Expense.
Shared Profiles

How do I use a shared profile?

  1. Open More.
  2. Open Shared Profiles.
  3. Create or join the appropriate shared profile, then follow the on-screen invitation flow.
FAQ

Beta tester questions.

How do I add a new order?

Open Orders, then tap Add New Order. Enter the purchase and rebate information shown in the form and save. See the How To section above for the exact Orders screen.

How do I find an order I already entered?

Open Orders and use the search field. You can search using details such as the item, order number, source, notes, or other identifying information shown by the app.

How do I see what an order is waiting on?

Open the order from the Orders list. Its Order Details screen shows the current status, timeline, expected refund information, and the next action that needs attention.

How do I record a refund that covers more than one order?

Open Orders and choose Record a Multi-Order Refund. Rebatify requires multiple eligible orders for this workflow, so a single order cannot be submitted as a multi-order refund.

How do I view spending, refunds, and Out Of Pocket Expense?

Open Reports. The Reports area gives you time-period views and breakdowns of spending, refunds, and Out Of Pocket Expense based on your recorded orders.

How do I submit beta feedback?

Use the Tell us what happened form above. Choose the feedback type, identify the page or feature, include your app build and device when possible, and describe what you expected versus what happened.

How often do I need to submit feedback?

There is no daily quota. We simply ask that you genuinely use Rebatify during the beta and submit meaningful feedback when you encounter an issue or have a useful suggestion.

Can I report something even if I am not sure it is a bug?

Yes. If something feels confusing, inconvenient, inconsistent, or difficult to use, submit it. That kind of feedback is valuable even when the software is technically working as designed.

Will negative feedback affect my beta access?

No. Honest feedback is encouraged. Constructive criticism is one of the main reasons the beta exists.

What happens if I stop testing?

Because beta availability is limited, inactive spots may eventually be reassigned to another person on the waitlist.

How do I sign in to the Rebatify Beta Program Portal?

Use the same email address that was approved for the Rebatify Beta Program. We will send a 6-digit verification code to that email. The portal does not use a separate password. If you cannot receive the code or need your portal access reset, email support.rebatifyapp@gmail.com.
