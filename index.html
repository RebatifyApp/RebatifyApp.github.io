How an order moves through Rebatify

Workflow first. Refund status second. Both matter.

A refund arriving does not automatically mean the physical/order workflow is finished. Rebatify keeps the obligation workflow and refund workflow independent, then archives an order when both sides meet the completion rules.

1
Add the orderChoose the source, shopping account, refund setup, Out Of Pocket Expense rule, and obligation. Rebatify calculates the expected refund due timing from the selected source.
2
Complete required stepsReceive the item and finish applicable review, rating, seller-feedback, delivery-confirmation, or group-form steps.
3
Record refunds as they arriveEnter one or multiple transactions, or allocate a grouped refund across several orders.
4
Finalize the outcomeFully refunded orders archive when workflow is done; partial outcomes can be explicitly completed without leaving the shortfall in pending cash.

Financial logic

“Cash Still Tied Up” means money you still expect back.

Rebatify intentionally does not calculate tied-up cash as purchase total minus refunds. It is the remaining expected refund on active orders. Expected Out Of Pocket Expense is excluded. If you complete an order with a partial refund, that remaining unrecovered shortfall is preserved as the final outcome but no longer counts as pending cash.

Security & devices

Device protection is standard, not a Rebatify+ add-on.

Every login has protected device access. Standard accounts use one primary-device access slot. Rebatify+ supports up to 3 trusted devices/clients per login, and web access counts as one of those same slots. When replacement rules apply, Rebatify uses a 24-hour replacement cooldown and 48-hour takeover/promotion protection. Device Management shows Primary, Trusted, Temporary, Not Active, This Device, platform, and last-active information when available.
