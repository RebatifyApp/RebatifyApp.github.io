Account & Privacy

Delete Your Rebatify Account & Data

You can permanently delete your Rebatify account and associated app data.

Fastest option: If you can sign in to Rebatify, delete your account directly in the app at More → Account & Security → Delete Rebatify Account.

Delete your account in the Rebatify app

  1. Open Rebatify and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open More.
  3. Select Account & Security.
  4. Select Delete Rebatify Account.
  5. Review the deletion notice and complete the confirmation steps shown in the app.

Once the deletion process is completed, your Rebatify authentication account and personal app data associated with that account are permanently removed, subject to the limited retention described below.

If you cannot access the app

You can request account deletion by emailing support.rebatifyapp@gmail.com. Send the request from the email address associated with your Rebatify account when possible and state that you want your Rebatify account and associated data deleted. We may need to verify that you control the account before completing a deletion request.

Data deleted with your account

Account deletion is intended to remove the personal Rebatify app data associated with your account, including:

  • Your Rebatify authentication account and account/profile information.
  • Your orders, rebate and refund tracking records, amounts, dates, statuses, notes, and related history.
  • Your Rebatify settings, saved sources, shopping or ordering accounts, refund methods/accounts, and other account-specific app configuration.
  • Your personal shared-membership/profile associations and other personal cloud data associated with your Rebatify account, subject to applicable shared-data ownership requirements.

Limited information that may be retained

Some limited records may be retained after account deletion when reasonably necessary for security, fraud or abuse prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our terms. In particular, device-bound trial-eligibility or anti-abuse records may be retained so deleting and recreating an account does not restart or bypass trial restrictions. These retained records are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Deleting your Rebatify app account does not automatically delete records associated with a separate Rebatify Beta Program application or participation record. If you also want Beta Program records deleted, include that request in your email to support.

Need help?

For account deletion or privacy assistance, contact support.rebatifyapp@gmail.com.
